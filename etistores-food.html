Our Story

Cooking from the Heart
Since 2015

Etistores Food was born out of a deep love for Akwa Ibom's rich culinary heritage. What started as a small family kitchen has grown into a beloved community dining destination in Osongama, Uyo.

Every dish is prepared fresh, every day, using locally sourced ingredients and time-honoured recipes. We believe food is more than nourishment — it's memory, culture, and connection.
